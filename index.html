Who We Are

US-Based Small Business Federal Contractor

Vector Systems Group LLC is a US-based limited liability company actively pursuing federal service contracts. Our headquarters is in Portsmouth, New Hampshire, and we serve customers nationwide. We specialize in environmental services and facilities support, and execute work directly or with a trusted subcontractor network when scope demands additional capacity.

Our approach is straightforward: clear scope confirmation, compliance-first execution, and rapid communication with contracting officers and prime contractors.
